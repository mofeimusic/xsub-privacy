xSubscribe — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-20 · Contact: 3587237300@qq.com

In short: xSubscribe has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. The subscriptions you enter stay on your iPhone. The developer receives none of your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your subscriptions, categories and account names (service names, prices, dates, usernames you type in) are stored in the app's database on your device.
  • Passwords and license keys you add to the vault are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only, protected by Face ID or your passcode. The app never receives your biometric data — iOS only tells it whether verification succeeded.
  • Settings such as your default currency are stored on the device.
  • A small summary for the Home Screen widget (upcoming renewal names, amounts and dates) is shared between the app and its widget through an App Group on your device.

What leaves your device

  • Exchange rates. To convert subscriptions in other currencies, the app asks the public service api.frankfurter.dev (European Central Bank reference rates) for a rate. The request contains only two currency codes and a date. It contains no subscription names, amounts, or identifiers. As with any web request, that service can see your IP address. Results are cached on your device.
  • Nothing else. The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and does not send your data to the developer or any other third party.

Permissions

  • Face ID / passcode — to lock the app and protect vault entries.
  • Notifications — local reminders scheduled on your device before renewals.
  • Calendars (optional) — only if you turn on calendar sync. The app then creates a calendar with repeating all-day renewal events, and removes them when you turn the option off.
  • Photos — the app only receives screenshots you choose with the system photo picker. Text in them is recognized on your device; the images are not saved or uploaded.

Backups and exports

Export creates a file (subscription names, prices, dates, categories, and the names and usernames of account entries) that you save wherever you choose. It never contains passwords or license keys. Keep it somewhere you trust. Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or a computer) may include the app's data according to your device settings; vault secrets are marked as this-device-only.

Deleting your data

Deleting entries in the app removes them. Deleting the app removes its database from your device. iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so delete vault entries in the app first if you want them gone.

Children

xSubscribe is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.

爱订阅 — 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-09-20 · 联系方式：3587237300@qq.com

简单来说：爱订阅没有账号体系，没有自己的服务器，没有广告，也没有统计分析。你录入的订阅只保存在你的 iPhone 上，开发者不会收到你的任何数据。

App 保存什么、保存在哪里

  • 订阅、类别和账号名称（服务名称、价格、日期、你输入的用户名）保存在这台设备上 App 自己的数据库里。
  • 你放进保险库的密码和注册码只保存在这台设备的 iOS 钥匙串里，由 Face ID 或锁屏密码保护。App 不会拿到你的生物特征数据，iOS 只会告诉它验证是否通过。
  • 设置（例如默认币种）保存在设备上。
  • 桌面小组件的摘要（即将续费的名称、金额和日期）通过设备上的 App Group 在 App 和小组件之间共享。

哪些内容会离开你的设备

  • 汇率。为了折算其他币种的订阅，App 会向公开服务 api.frankfurter.dev（欧洲央行参考汇率）查询汇率。请求里只有两个币种代码和一个日期，不包含订阅名称、金额或任何标识信息。和任何网络请求一样，对方可以看到你的 IP 地址。查询结果会缓存在设备上。
  • 除此之外没有。App 没有统计分析、广告或跟踪，也不会把你的数据发送给开发者或任何第三方。

权限说明

  • Face ID / 锁屏密码——用于锁定 App 和保护保险库条目。
  • 通知——在设备本地安排的续费前提醒。
  • 日历（可选）——仅在你开启日历同步时使用。App 会新建一个日历，写入重复的全天续费事件，关闭该选项时会一并删除。
  • 照片——App 只会收到你用系统相册选择器选中的截图。图中的文字在设备本地识别，图片不会被保存或上传。

备份与导出

导出会生成一个文件（订阅名称、价格、日期、类别，以及账号条目的名称和用户名），保存在你选择的位置，不包含密码和注册码，请妥善保管。你 iPhone 自带的备份（iCloud 或电脑）可能会按你的设备设置包含本 App 的数据；保险库里的密码被标记为仅限本设备。

删除你的数据

在 App 里删除条目即可移除它们；删除 App 会移除设备上的数据库。iOS 可能在 App 被删除后仍保留钥匙串条目，如果希望彻底清除，请先在 App 里删除保险库条目。

儿童

爱订阅不面向儿童，也不会收集任何人的个人信息。

政策变更

如果本政策有变更，新版本会发布在本页面，并更新生效日期。
